# Senso4s

Never out of gas. Senso4s devices keep track of the gas level in your cylinder and help forecast when it is time for a refill, so your barbecue, camping trip, patio heater or next adventure stays fueled.

Pair your Senso4s sensor with Homey to monitor gas level, remaining gas, battery status and device warnings from your favorite automations.
